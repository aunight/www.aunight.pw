關於我About Me

我是一名自由接案者，從事影片、設計與音樂相關工作至今8年。I'm a freelance creator with 8 years of experience across video, design and music.

影片剪輯與後製Editing & VFX

  • 我從高中開始自學影片剪輯，接案至今已8年。I taught myself video editing in high school and have worked freelance for 8 years since.
  • 我曾與阿神、尊、放火、阿滴等百萬訂閱YouTuber合作，第一支為阿神剪輯的影片便突破百萬觀看。I've collaborated with million-subscriber YouTubers such as 阿神, 尊, 放火 and 阿滴 — the first video I edited for 阿神 surpassed a million views.
  • 除了剪輯，我也製作YouTube縮圖與平面設計，擅長提升點閱表現。Beyond editing, I also produce YouTube thumbnails and graphic design, with a focus on click-through.
  • 在商業專案中，我也使用Fusion處理動態追蹤等特效需求。For commercial projects, I also handle motion-tracking and other VFX in Fusion.

UI/UX與產品設計UI/UX & Product Design

  • 我不只專注於視覺，後來也跨入UI/UX領域。I didn't stop at visuals — I later moved into UI/UX as well.
  • 我負責設計的VALKI（Valorant輔助工具）目前服務超過10萬名用戶，官方網站也由我設計。I led the design of VALKI, a Valorant utility now serving 100,000+ users, and designed its official website too.
  • 我也以side project形式，為巴哈姆特（首頁、動畫瘋、社群）、台北捷運Go、Dcard等App與網站進行概念改版設計。As personal side projects, I've also created redesign concepts for apps and sites such as Bahamut (homepage, Anime Crazy, community), Taipei MRT Go and Dcard.

音樂製作Music Production

  • 我同時是一名音樂製作人，至今創作超過30首原創歌曲與BGM。I'm also a music producer, with 30+ original songs and BGM tracks to date.
  • 我曾為Vtuber製作原創歌曲，從作詞、作曲、編曲到混音皆由我完成，作品也發行於Spotify與Apple Music。I've produced original songs for Vtubers, handling everything from lyrics and composition to arrangement and mixing, released on Spotify and Apple Music.
  • 加上我也製作PV動畫，因此能獨力完成從聲音到畫面的整體製作。I also produce PV animations, so I can deliver the full package from sound to visuals on my own.

由我一人整合製作One Creator, End to End

單一窗口溝通，讓畫面與聲音不再各自為政。

A single point of contact, keeping your visuals and sound aligned.

  • 不用拆成三邊溝通 剪輯、設計、音樂都可以直接找我對，一個窗口就能整理方向。
  • 從畫面到聲音一起做 影片、網站、配樂或PV可以用同一套標準處理，不會各做各的。
  • 修改也比較直接 節奏、視覺、音樂要調整時，不用來回轉述，整體比較容易統一。
  • No split communication Editing, design and music can all be discussed through one contact.
  • Visuals and sound together Video, websites, music and PVs can follow one shared direction.
  • Cleaner revisions Pacing, visuals and music are easier to adjust without relaying feedback.

Why work with one person?

為什麼選擇交給同一人？

  • One consistent style The pacing fits the music, and the design fits the code.
  • Less back-and-forth One contact who sees the whole project, instead of three.
  • Better value A bundled rate beats hiring several freelancers or an agency.
  • 風格一致 影像節奏與音樂相互呼應，設計與程式也彼此契合。
  • 溝通單純 單一窗口即可掌握整個專案，省去三方來回協調。
  • 成本更省 整合報價比分別發案或委託團隊更為划算。
